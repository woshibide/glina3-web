Text:

At GLINA³, we are open to commissions for custom ceramic pieces designed specifically for restaurants, cafes, bars, hotels, and art spaces. Whether you're looking for distinctive tableware, sculptural objects, lighting elements, or site-specific installations, we create ceramics that bring a tactile and thoughtful presence to your environment.

Working with ceramic 3D printing and hand-finishing techniques, we produce small-batch and made-to-measure pieces that reflect the unique character of each space. We value collaboration and are committed to developing work that aligns with your vision, whether functional or decorative.

If you're a business or cultural space interested in integrating modern ceramics into your setting or experience, we'd love to hear from you: (email: office@glina3.nl)
