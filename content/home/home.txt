Title: Pick a flower

----

Text:

PICK A FLOWER is GLINA³'s first collection and the starting point of our ornament research in contemporary life.

We began with embroidery archives and pattern books, noticing how floral motifs appear across cultures as a shared visual language. The flowers were rewritten through a digital lens and translated into clay via additive manufacturing.

The title points to a modern condition: we live inside endless options, yet choice can be strangely illusory. Sometimes the only honest gesture is simple: pick a flower, and let life carry the story.
